Indian law, in plain language, with citations

Ask about your rights under Indian law. I search the Constitution, about 1,000 central Acts, and the 2024 criminal codes (BNS, BNSS, BSA) that replaced the IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act — then cite the exact section.

For procedures I can also read official government sites for the current fee, deadline and portal. Ask in English, Hindi, or Hinglish.
