2What happens every time

  • Safety first. If you describe violence, self-harm or an arrest in progress, helpline numbers appear before anything else.
  • The law is searched, not remembered. By meaning and by keyword, then re-ranked, across the Constitution, ~1,000 central Acts and the 2023 codes.
  • Every citation is checked. A citation that does not match a source it was given is removed before you see the answer.
  • It says when it doesn’t know. If nothing in the law is close enough, it tells you so instead of guessing.
  • Where you are matters. State and Union Territory laws are labelled; for state subjects such as rent, set your state with the pin below the question box.
